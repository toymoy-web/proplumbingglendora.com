A College Town With Deep Historic Character

Claremont is home to the seven Claremont Colleges, including Pomona College, Scripps College, and Harvey Mudd College, and the city's residential character reflects that academic history: tree-lined streets, numerous historic homes, and a strong emphasis on preserving the city's architectural heritage. Homes closer to the college campuses and the historic downtown shopping district, known locally simply as the Village, are more likely to carry original plumbing from the early 20th century.

Claremont's historic downtown Village should not be confused with Glendora Village, the similarly-named historic district in our own home city. Both are real, separate downtown districts with their own local identity.

Water Supply

Hard Water Consistent With the Broader Valley

Claremont draws on the same general San Gabriel Valley and imported water supply mix found throughout this part of Los Angeles County, commonly testing in the moderately hard to hard range. That mineral content drives the same sediment and scale pattern we treat on water heaters and fixtures throughout our service area.

Local Identity

The Easternmost City in Our Service Area

Claremont sits at the eastern edge of Los Angeles County, bordering San Bernardino County, which makes it the farthest reach of our regular service area. Its direct connection to La Verne, which we also serve, keeps it within a realistic dispatch radius from our Glendora base.

Landmarks

The Claremont Colleges and Historic Downtown

The seven Claremont Colleges form a significant part of the city's identity and geography, with a mix of campus-adjacent rental housing and long-time family neighborhoods surrounding them. The historic downtown Village, a collection of shops, restaurants, and galleries just west of the colleges, anchors much of the city's commercial district and sits among some of Claremont's oldest residential streets.

FAQs

Questions From Claremont

Do you charge extra to come to Claremont?

No, Claremont is inside our regular service area, reached via the SR-210 corridor through La Verne.

Is my Claremont home likely to have original plumbing?

If it's near the historic downtown Village or the Claremont Colleges and predates the mid-20th century, there's a real chance of original supply lines. We can confirm during an inspection.

Is Claremont's Village the same as Glendora Village?

No, they're two separate historic downtown districts in two different cities. Claremont's Village sits near the Claremont Colleges, while Glendora Village is our own home city's historic downtown.

Do you work near the Claremont Colleges, or just residential areas?

Both. We handle residential plumbing throughout Claremont, including neighborhoods near all seven of the Claremont Colleges.

How big is Claremont, and does that affect response time?

Claremont's population was about 37,300 at the 2020 census. As the easternmost city in our service area, response times are slightly longer than for our closer coverage areas, but still well within a realistic dispatch radius.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Claremont?

Yes. Claremont is served by a single ZIP code, 91711, covering the entire city from the historic downtown Village through the neighborhoods surrounding the Claremont Colleges. Every address in that ZIP falls inside our standard service area.

Is emergency service available in Claremont?

Yes, our 24/7 emergency line covers Claremont the same as our closer service areas. A burst pipe or active leak gets a dispatched response any hour of the day.
