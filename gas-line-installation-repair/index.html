Overview

Gas Line Work Done to Code, Every Time

Gas line work isn't a place to cut corners, and every job we do in Glendora, whether it's running a new line for an outdoor kitchen, connecting a tankless water heater, or repairing a leak, is pressure-tested and brought up to current code before we call it finished. If you smell gas, that's always treated as an emergency call, not a scheduled appointment.

Common jobs include extending a line for a new gas range, fireplace, or outdoor kitchen; upsizing an undersized line that's starving an appliance of proper pressure; and diagnosing and repairing leaks found either by smell, a failed pressure test, or a utility company notice.

Why This Happens in Glendora

What Drives Gas Line Calls in Glendora

A number of Glendora's original 1940s and 1950s homes still have steel gas lines sized for a much smaller household gas load than today's appliances demand, a situation that surfaces when a homeowner adds an outdoor kitchen, a tankless water heater, or a gas fireplace insert and finds the existing line can't support the added draw without an upgrade.

The same alluvial soil movement that stresses water lines in this area affects buried gas lines too, particularly at fixed connection points, which is one of several reasons a professional pressure test matters even on a line that looks fine on the surface.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Gas Line Service

Smell of Gas

Hissing Sound Near a Connection

Worth an immediate professional inspection.

Dead or Discolored Vegetation Near a Buried Line

A possible sign of an underground gas leak.

Pilot Light Issues Across Multiple Appliances

Can indicate a supply-side problem rather than individual appliance faults.

Planning a New Gas Appliance

Worth confirming your existing line can support the added load before installation.

Repair Options

Repair vs. Line Upgrade

Repair or Spot Fix

  • Addresses a specific leak or damaged section
  • Right call when the existing line is otherwise adequately sized
  • Lower cost and faster turnaround

Line Upgrade or Extension

  • Needed when adding a new appliance the existing line can't support
  • Resolves recurring low-output issues at appliances
  • Brings older, undersized lines up to current demand and code

FAQs

Common Questions

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the area immediately, don't operate any switches or appliances, and call from a safe distance. Our emergency line runs 24/7 for exactly this situation.

Can my existing gas line support a new appliance?

It depends on the line's current size and what's already drawing from it. We can assess your existing setup and tell you honestly whether an upgrade is needed before you purchase a new appliance.

Do you pull permits for gas line work?

Yes, gas line installation and significant repairs typically require a permit and inspection, which we handle as part of the job.

How long does a gas line repair take?

Most single-point repairs are completed same-day. A line extension or upsizing project may take longer depending on the length and access involved.
