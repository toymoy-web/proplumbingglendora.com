Overview

The Line Between the Street and Your House

The water line, sometimes called the service line, is the underground pipe that carries water from the meter or curb stop to your home, distinct from the interior plumbing a repiping job addresses. A problem here shows up differently than an inside leak: a wet patch in the yard or along the sidewalk, a spinning meter with everything in the house off, or a drop in pressure affecting every fixture at once rather than just one area.

We confirm the leak is actually on the service line, rather than inside the house, using pressure isolation at the meter before recommending any repair. From there, the fix depends on the pipe material and how much of the line is affected, a single point repair, or in some cases a full trenchless replacement of the line.

Why This Happens in Glendora

Why Service Lines Fail in Glendora

Many older Glendora properties, particularly near the Village and along the Route 66 corridor, still have original galvanized service lines that corrode from the inside over decades. It's the same process that affects interior galvanized pipe but happening underground where it isn't visible until pressure drops or a leak surfaces at the yard.

The foothill area's alluvial soil compounds the problem for every pipe material, not just galvanized. Seasonal expansion and contraction stresses a buried line along its entire length, and a fixed connection point at the meter or the house is often where that stress eventually causes a crack or a joint separation.

Repair Options

Spot Repair vs. Full Line Replacement

Spot Repair

  • A single failure point in an otherwise healthy line.
  • Lower cost and faster when damage is contained to one point.
  • Right call on newer copper or PEX service lines.

Full Trenchless Replacement

  • Recommended for aging or fully galvanized service lines.
  • Addresses the underlying corrosion rather than one symptom of it.
  • Minimal digging compared to full open-trench replacement.

FAQs

Common Questions

How do I know if the leak is on my service line or inside my house?

The clearest test is your water meter: shut off every fixture inside, and if the meter is still moving, the leak is on the service line between the meter and the house, not inside.

Can you replace a service line without digging up my whole yard?

In many cases, yes, trenchless replacement pulls a new line through the path of the old one with minimal digging. Whether it's an option depends on the line's path and soil conditions, which we assess before quoting.

Is my water line the city's responsibility or mine?

Typically, the portion from the water meter to your home is the homeowner's responsibility, while the city maintains the main up to the meter. We can help clarify where your specific issue falls.

Why is my water bill high with no visible leak?

A service line leak underground often shows no visible sign inside the house. A spinning meter with everything off, or a steadily climbing bill, is usually the first real clue.
