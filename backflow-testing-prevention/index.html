Overview

Protecting Glendora's Water Supply

A backflow preventer stops contaminated water from being siphoned backward into the clean water supply, a real risk on irrigation systems, commercial properties, and any setup where a cross-connection exists. Many Glendora properties with irrigation systems or commercial fixtures are required to have their preventer tested annually, and we handle both the testing and any repair or replacement the test uncovers.

If your preventer fails testing, we can typically repair or replace it the same day, then retest to confirm it passes before we leave.

Why This Happens in Glendora

Why Backflow Testing Matters Here

Glendora's mix of established landscaping and irrigation systems, particularly on larger foothill properties and commercial sites along the Route 66 corridor, means a meaningful share of local properties have a backflow preventer somewhere on the irrigation or fire suppression system, and most local water purveyors, including the City of Glendora Water Department, require annual testing to keep those devices certified.

The area's hard water and mineral content can also affect internal preventer components over time, similar to the wear pattern seen on other fixtures, another reason routine testing catches problems before they become a compliance issue or an actual cross-connection risk.

Properties on the western edge of Glendora served by Suburban Water Systems, rather than the City of Glendora Water Department directly, should confirm which purveyor's testing schedule and reporting requirements apply to their specific address, since the certification process can vary slightly between the two.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often do I need backflow testing?

Most local water purveyors, including the City of Glendora Water Department, require annual testing for irrigation and commercial backflow preventers. We can confirm your specific requirement.

What happens if my device fails the test?

We can typically repair or replace it the same day and retest before we leave, so you don't need a separate return visit in most cases.

Do you file the certification paperwork for me?

Yes, we submit the passing test report to the required water purveyor or agency on your behalf.

Do I need a backflow preventer if I don't have irrigation?

It depends on your property's plumbing setup, certain fixtures, fire suppression systems, and commercial connections can also require one. We can assess your property if you're not sure.

Which water purveyor determines my testing requirements?

Most of Glendora is served by the City of Glendora Water Department, with some western areas served by Suburban Water Systems. We can confirm which applies to your address and its specific reporting schedule.
