Where to Start

Not Sure Which Page You Need?

Most calls fall into a handful of categories. If your water heater is making noise or has stopped producing hot water, start with water heater repair. If a drain is slow or backed up, drain cleaning is usually the right page, and if the same line keeps clogging, hydro jetting goes further than a standard snake. A rising water bill with no obvious cause points to leak detection, and a warm patch on a slab floor specifically points to slab leak detection instead. If you're not sure which of these describes your situation, call (626) 898-6169 and describe what you're seeing , we'll point you to the right service.

How We Price Work

Upfront Pricing on Every Service

Every service listed above gets the same treatment: a technician diagnoses the actual problem, gives you a firm price before touching anything, and explains repair versus replacement honestly rather than defaulting to whichever costs more. Pricing factors mentioned on individual service pages are general guidance, the specific number depends on what a technician finds on-site, which is why we quote a real number after assessment rather than a number over the phone.

Same-Day and Emergency Service

When You Need It Fast

Active emergencies, a burst pipe, a sewage backup, no hot water in a household that needs it , go on our 24/7 emergency line, and a technician is dispatched as quickly as we can manage, day or night. Non-emergency repairs and installations, like a new water heater or a bathroom fixture upgrade, typically get a same-day or next-day appointment window instead. If you're not sure which category your situation falls into, call (626) 898-6169 and describe it, we'll tell you honestly.

Repair vs. Replacement

We Tell You Which One You Actually Need

Nearly every service on this page has a genuine repair-vs-replacement decision behind it, and each service page walks through both sides honestly, when a water heater is worth repairing versus replacing, when a sewer line needs a spot fix versus a full lateral replacement, when a faucet just needs a new cartridge versus a full swap. We diagnose first and quote second, which means the answer depends on what a technician actually finds at your address, not a default recommendation.

Licensed Work, Every Time

C-36 Licensed Plumbing Contractors

Every service on this page, from a routine faucet repair to a full sewer line replacement, is performed under California's C-36 plumbing contractor classification. Gas line work and certain installations are permitted and inspected as required. Learn more about how we work on our about page, or just call (626) 898-6169 with any question before you decide.
