Overview

When a Snake Isn't Enough

Hydro jetting uses a high-pressure water stream, delivered through a specialized nozzle, to scour the full interior wall of a drain or sewer line clean, not just punch a channel through whatever's blocking it. We recommend it in Glendora most often for main sewer lines with recurring backups, kitchen lines with years of grease buildup, and older clay or cast-iron laterals affected by root intrusion.

It's a more thorough process than standard cable snaking, and it's not always the right first step for a simple, isolated clog. We'll recommend it when the situation actually calls for it, a line that keeps clogging after snaking, visible grease or scale buildup on a camera inspection, or root intrusion that a cable would only temporarily clear.

Why This Happens in Glendora

Why Glendora Lines Need Jetting More Than Some Areas

Glendora's older neighborhoods, particularly near the Village and along the historic Route 66 corridor, still run original clay or cast-iron sewer laterals in many homes. The mature landscaping the city is known for, including its signature ficus trees, sends roots into any crack or joint in those older lines looking for moisture, and a cable snake typically just cuts a path through the root mass rather than removing it. This is why the same line clogs again within weeks or months.

On the fixture side, the area's 150 to 220 ppm hard water builds mineral scale on pipe walls over time, which reduces how much open cross-section is left for grease and soap scum to grab onto in the to. Jetting clears both the scale and the buildup it traps, which is why it tends to hold longer than a standard clearing on lines that have gone years without deeper maintenance.

Repair Options

Snaking vs. Hydro Jetting

Cable Snaking

  • Fast, effective for a single isolated clog
  • Lower cost for routine blockages
  • Cuts through the obstruction rather than clearing the pipe wall

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears grease, scale, and root remnants from the full pipe wall
  • Longer-lasting results on recurring or older-pipe clogs
  • Recommended when snaking hasn't held or a camera shows heavy buildup

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

We check the pipe material and condition with a camera first. For already-compromised clay or cast-iron lines, we'll recommend the safest effective method rather than jetting by default.

How is jetting different from snaking?

A snake cuts a channel through a clog. Jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire interior wall of the pipe clean. This is why it holds longer against grease, scale, and root regrowth.

Can jetting remove tree roots permanently?

Jetting clears root intrusion far more thoroughly than snaking, but if a crack or joint separation is letting roots in, they can return over time. A camera inspection tells us whether the line also needs a repair, not just cleaning.

Do you provide a camera inspection with jetting?

Yes, we typically inspect before and after jetting to confirm the actual condition of the line and verify the clean was successful.
