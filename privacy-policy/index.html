Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glendora Pro Plumbing ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website, proplumbingglendora.com.

Information We Collect

This site does not use contact forms. Any information you share with us, such as your name, phone number, or address, is provided directly when you call or text (626) 898-6169, and is used only to schedule and complete the plumbing service you've requested. Like most websites, we may also collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type, general location, and pages visited, through standard analytics tools.

How We Use Information

Information collected during a service call is used to schedule appointments, provide the requested plumbing service, and for related recordkeeping such as invoicing. Automatically collected technical information is used to understand how visitors use the site and to improve its content and performance.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with third-party service providers who help us operate our business (such as call tracking or scheduling tools), and as required by law.

Third-Party Links

This site may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their own policies.
