What to Expect

What Happens After You Call

Describe what you're dealing with and we'll tell you honestly what it sounds like and roughly what to expect, before anyone comes out. For an active emergency, a burst pipe, no hot water, a sewage backup, we dispatch as quickly as we can, day or night. For a non-emergency repair, most callers get a same-day or next-day appointment window. Either way, a technician diagnoses the actual problem on-site and gives you a firm, upfront price before any work begins, not an estimate that changes once the job is underway.

If you're not sure whether what you're describing needs a same-day call or can wait, just ask , we'd rather tell you honestly than have you sit on a real problem or pay for emergency dispatch you didn't need.

Before You Call

A Few Things That Speed Up the Call

It helps to know roughly which fixture or area is affected, whether water is actively flowing or just slow to drain, and whether you've already located your main shutoff valve in case the situation worsens before we arrive. None of that is required to call, we'll walk you through diagnosing the situation over the phone either way, but having a rough sense of the problem helps us bring the right parts on the first visit.
