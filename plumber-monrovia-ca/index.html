Old Town Monrovia and the Surrounding Foothills

Monrovia's historic Old Town district, centered on Myrtle Avenue, retains much of its late-19th and early-20th-century character, and homes in and around that core are among the most likely in our service area to still carry original galvanized supply piping. Further from downtown, Monrovia's hillside neighborhoods climbing toward the San Gabriel Mountains reflect later construction eras with copper supply lines instead.

That mix means a Monrovia service call can range from a galvanized-to-copper repipe on a Craftsman-era Old Town home to a slab leak on a newer hillside property, similar to the pattern we already navigate in Azusa and Duarte.

Water Supply

Monrovia's Own Municipal Water Department

Monrovia runs its own municipal water utility, the Monrovia City Water Department, drawing primarily on local groundwater. Like most municipal groundwater supplies in this part of the San Gabriel Valley, it commonly tests in the moderately hard range, contributing to the same sediment and scale pattern we treat on water heaters and fixtures throughout our service area.

Local Identity

West of Duarte, Along the Foothills

Monrovia sits just west of Duarte along the same foothill corridor at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains, close enough to our existing Duarte and Azusa coverage that it extends naturally from our regular service area. The city shares the same seasonal alluvial soil movement that drives cracked and ruptured pipe calls throughout this stretch of the valley.

Landmarks

Historic Old Town and the Gold Line Station

Old Town Monrovia's Myrtle Avenue corridor, known for its weekly Family Festival street fair, anchors the city's commercial and historic identity, surrounded by some of the oldest residential streets in our service area. The Monrovia Gold Line station connects the city to the broader Los Angeles rail network, and new transit-adjacent development near the station reflects more recent construction standards than the surrounding historic neighborhoods.

Questions From Monrovia

Do you charge extra to come to Monrovia?

No, Monrovia is inside our regular service area, extending naturally from our existing Duarte and Azusa coverage along the foothill corridor.

Is my Monrovia home likely to have galvanized pipe?

If it's in or near Old Town Monrovia and predates the 1960s, there's a real chance of original galvanized supply lines. We can confirm during an inspection.

Does the Monrovia City Water Department handle service line issues, or is that on me?

Typically the portion from the meter to your home is the homeowner's responsibility, while the city maintains up to the meter. We can help clarify your specific situation.

Do you work on both Old Town homes and the newer hillside properties?

Yes, we regularly work across Monrovia's full range of housing eras, from the historic Old Town core to the newer hillside neighborhoods toward the foothills.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Monrovia?

Is emergency service available in Monrovia?
